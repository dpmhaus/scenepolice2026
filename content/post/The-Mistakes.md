High-energy punk rock band from Poole, UK.

"Die Laughing,“ THE MISTAKES’ fifth studio record and their most explosive work to date, is packed with rousing, in-your-face punk anthems. It delivers pounding drums, pumping basslines, riotous riffage, and choruses built for mass singalongs.

Across 13 electrifying tracks, The Mistakes combine razor-sharp arrangements with their trademark lyrical mix of wit, sarcasm, defiance, and devilment, everything you want from a punk rock record and then some.
